Preferences

The settings actually in force on this site. An information page, read-only: nothing is configured here.

Languages

SettingValue
Reference languageFrench — runs are written in the language of the request, French by default.
Available languagesFrench and English. Language is derived from the URL: French at the root, English under /en/. The switcher sits in the footer.
Default viewEnglish on the home page and on the “e-commerce personalization” market; French everywhere else. A provisional setting (6 September 2026): French stays reachable through the FR link in the footer, and that choice is then remembered.
Translated pagesThe home page, the documentation and its four focus pages, the corporate pages, the “weight-loss apps” market (6 runs out of 7) and the “e-commerce personalization” market (market page + run S1). An untranslated page falls back to its French version rather than to an empty page.

Access and visibility

SettingValue
AccessEntirely private since 18 September 2026: every page, every dataset and every stylesheet goes through the access control shared by all projects. No public page. Permissions are managed elsewhere, in the accounts area.
Indexingnoindex, nofollow, noarchive on every response, redirects included. The site appears in no search engine.
Audience measurementCloudflare Web Analytics, cookieless and without any personal identifier. Measurement is disabled locally, so working previews never skew the figures.
Cookies setOne only, functional: the language preference, when a reader clicks “FR” or “EN”. No advertising or tracking cookie.

Content covered

SettingValue
Markets coveredFour: mobile weight-loss assistance apps · greeting & social cards (United States) · micro-ticket seed capital · e-commerce personalization SaaS tools.
Runs publishedThirteen, split 7 · 1 · 1 · 4. Two markets have been through all four pipeline steps; two have step 1 only.
CadenceNo automatic schedule: every run is triggered on demand. A run is never modified once published.
Reference periodChosen run by run (6 months, 1 year or 3 years) according to the pace of the market, and justified in the report.

Writing conventions

SettingValue
Estimated valuesA “~” prefix on every estimate; “—” when the figure could not be found. An estimate is never presented as an established number.
UnitsSpelled out — “billion $”, “million $” — never abbreviated, to rule out any misreading.
GlossesA defined term takes French quotation marks « … » in French and italics in English. Acronyms are spelled out at first use.
Reading widthA fluid column that follows the width of the screen, identical across all run pages (decision of 6 September 2026).
TypefacesHanken Grotesk (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels and figures), Instrument Serif (framework questions).

Data

SettingValue
Structured dataKept: each run carries its own data canvas, versioned alongside its report. No database.
Raw dataNot kept: the pages and articles consulted are discarded after the run. Traceability runs entirely through the sources, referenced and dated in the appendix.
Personal dataNone is collected by the site.
HostingOVH (France). Fully static site: no server-side processing beyond access control.
